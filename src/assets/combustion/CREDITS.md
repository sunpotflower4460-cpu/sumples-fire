# Flame footage

Fire Task's 22 flame treatments use the following CC0 1.0 source recordings by Mostafa Meraji.

- [Fire آتش 01.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_آتش_01.ogv)
- [Fire آتش 07.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_آتش_07.ogv)
- [Fire آتش 08.ogv](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_آتش_08.ogv)

The flame was isolated from the surroundings, stabilized at the base, graded and edited into short loops. Some treatments combine independently timed takes. The small sparks in the firefly, steel and star-rain treatments come from isolated embers in the same recordings. The still for each flame comes from its loop.

The coal bed is a cropped and masked detail from Peter Forster's [Glowing Embers](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Glowing_Embers_(Unsplash).jpg), also released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
